<Note>
The Pages/Topics selector and context meter are part of the updated **Question Studio**, currently in development. This guide applies when those controls are available in your version.
</Note>

**Context** is the source text Question Studio can use for your questions. Choose material that covers what you want to test; you do not need to fill the bar.

## Choose your source

- **Topics:** select the concepts you want to cover. Their source pages form the context.
- **Pages:** open **Choose pages**, preview the thumbnails, and use the circles to select pages. You can also enter ranges such as `3–12, 24, 30–35`.

Opening a preview does not select it. **Selected only** lets you check your selection before clicking **Done**. Question Studio remembers your Pages/Topics preference for your account in the same browser.

## Read the meter

The number beside **Context** estimates the tokens in your selected source text. Tokens are small pieces of text; the estimate uses roughly four characters per token. Equations, OCR formatting, and language can change the actual count.

Pages shared by several selected topics count once. The bar uses a **20k-token scale**, with a suggested band from **3k to 10k**. It turns amber above 10k. Larger selections remain allowed, and the number continues to show the full estimate after the bar fills.

This is a selection guide, not the model's context-window usage or a quality score. Question Studio handles larger selections in smaller batches and gives each question relevant excerpts. The total selection is not sent in every prompt.

## How much should you select?

For a focused run of **5–10 questions**, start with:

| Selected source text | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Under 3k tokens | Often enough for a narrow topic. Add material only if it is needed. |
| 3k–10k tokens | A practical starting range for a focused run. |
| 10k–20k tokens | A broader scope; check that your question count can cover it. |
| Over 20k tokens | Consider separate runs by topic or section. |

These are starting points, not hard limits or guarantees. Page count alone is less useful: a dense textbook page can contain much more text than a slide.

## Check what the model can read

Use **Text used for questions** in the page preview to inspect the extracted text. Original thumbnails help you recognize pages, but questions cannot reliably use visual details that are missing from the extraction.

Review the generated questions and their source citations before saving them. More context does not automatically produce better questions.
