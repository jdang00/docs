## Open Class Progress

Choose **Class Progress** from the admin dashboard or open `/admin/progress`.

The workspace has four views:

- **Overview**
- **Classes**
- **Students**
- **Studio**

The URL preserves the selected view, filters, class, and module so drill-downs remain navigable.

## Overview

The overview is a cohort pulse check. Its top metrics are:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Students in cohort** | Current cohort members returned to the workspace. |
| **Active this week** | Tracked students with recorded activity in the last seven days. |
| **Median coverage** | The median share of the question bank tried by tracked students. |
| **Not started** | Tracked students with no questions tried. |

The **Coverage distribution** groups students into **Not started**, **Under 25%**, **25–74%**, and **75–100%**. Select a band to open the matching student list.

The overview also shows:

- **Least explored modules**, ranked by low question coverage
- students with zero questions tried
- students who started but have been inactive for at least 30 days
- recent student activity

<Warning>
Coverage is the share of available questions tried. Accuracy, mastery, and exam performance require separate evidence.
</Warning>

See [Reading progress analytics](/admin/reading-progress-analytics) for metric definitions and interpretation.

## Classes

The **Classes** view is a curriculum explorer.

1. Filter classes by semester or search by class name or code.
2. Open a class to see its modules.
3. Open a module for detailed participation and question-level coverage.

Each module detail shows:

- **Participation** — students who tried at least one question in the module
- **Question coverage** — completed student–question pairs out of all possible pairs
- **Flagged questions** and total student flags
- questions nobody has tried
- recent activity

The module detail tabs are:

- **Questions** — paginated question coverage, flags, and last interaction
- **Participants** — recently active participants and their tried/flagged counts
- **Flags** — up to 20 questions with the most student flags
- **Activity** — up to 20 recent student-question interactions

Use **Manage module** to move from analytics back into the authoring surface.

## Students

The **Students** view supports:

- search by name, email, or username
- sorting by recent activity, highest coverage, or name
- filtering by **Active this week**, **Quiet for 30+ days**, **Not started**, or a selected coverage band
- coverage, questions tried, last-active time, join date, and role badges

Open a student to see their identity, subscription badge when applicable, overall question activity, flagged count, and progress by semester, class, and module.

## Studio

The **Studio** view monitors recent Question Studio activity for the cohort.

Summary metrics include:

- runs this week and failures
- questions kept and saved into modules
- spend and cost per kept question when cost data is available
- median run duration

The recent-runs table shows the source document, destination module, curator, drafted versus requested count, kept count, cost, duration, and status. Cost can be approximate when provider billing data is estimated.

## Manage roles from a student profile

Role controls appear only when both the viewer and target are eligible.

### Admins

Admins can switch another user in the same cohort between:

- **Student**
- **Curator**

The admin role editor is limited to other students and curators in the same cohort. Self-management, admin/developer targets, and `admin` or `dev` assignments are excluded.

### Developers

Developers can use the profile UI to assign **Student**, **Curator**, or **Admin** to users other than themselves. Developer roles are handled outside this UI for security.

### Curators and students

Curators and students receive no role-management controls.

## Connection state

The workspace uses live data. If its WebSocket connection drops after connecting, LearnTerms warns that displayed data may be out of date and resumes updates automatically after reconnection.
