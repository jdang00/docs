---
title: "Question highlighting"
description: "Mark important wording in a question stem and keep the highlights for later review."
---

Turn on **Highlight** from the module **Tools** area. Press `H` to toggle highlight mode from the keyboard.

## Add and remove highlights

1. Turn on **Highlight**.
2. Select text in the question stem.
3. Release the pointer or finish the keyboard selection. LearnTerms applies and saves the highlight.

Click an existing highlight to remove it. Press `Ctrl+Z` on Windows or `Command+Z` on macOS to undo a highlight change made during the current study session.

Highlighting applies to the question stem. It does not mark answer choices or the rationale.

## Saved behavior

- Highlights are private to your account.
- They are saved by question and appear when you return.
- The on/off preference follows your account.
- A question can hold up to 80 saved highlight ranges.
- If a contributor changes the stem, LearnTerms hides highlights from the older version instead of placing them on the wrong text.

## Why use it

- Isolate qualifiers such as *most likely*, *except*, or *first*.
- Mark values, units, time course, and laterality in clinical stems.
- Reduce rereading during long questions.
- Leave visual anchors for later review without changing the question.

Highlight only the words that change the decision. Marking most of the stem removes the benefit.
