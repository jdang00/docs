Open **Calculator** from the module **Tools** area. You can also open the standalone calculator at [learnterms.com/tools/calculator](https://learnterms.com/tools/calculator).

## Enter a calculation

- Type in the expression field or use the keypad.
- Use `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `ln`, `log`, square root, powers, and `π` as needed.
- Switch **DEG** and **RAD** before using trigonometric functions.
- Read the result as you type.
- Click the result to copy it.
- Press `Enter` or click `=` to add the result to **History**.

**Ans** inserts the newest history result into the current expression.

## Use a formula

Select **Formulas**, then choose one of the built-in formulas:

| Formula | Inputs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spherical equivalent | Sphere and cylinder | Diopters |
| Prentice's rule | Decentration and lens power | Prism diopters |
| Vertex conversion | Spectacle power and vertex distance | Corneal-plane power |
| Focal length to power | Signed focal length | Diopters |
| Percentage | Part and whole | Percent |

Enter the labeled values. The displayed equation fills in and recalculates as the values change. Keep the prescription sign when a formula asks for a signed value.

## History and saved state

- History keeps up to 30 calculations.
- Click a history expression to reuse it.
- Click a history result to copy it.
- Select **Clear** under **History** to remove the list.
- The current expression, selected formula, formula inputs, angle mode, and history stay in local browser storage.

Calculator state is browser-specific. It does not sync through your LearnTerms account.

## Why use it

- Keep the question visible while calculating.
- Reduce copying between LearnTerms and another app.
- Reuse a result without retyping it.
- Use labeled optometry formulas to reduce unit and transcription mistakes.

The calculator supports the work. You still need to choose the correct formula, units, signs, and clinical interpretation.
